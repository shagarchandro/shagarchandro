Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Short version

This site doesn't have a server or database. Everything it stores lives entirely in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to, or collected by, any third party through the site itself.

What's stored, and where

  • Theme and language preference — remembered in your browser so you don't have to reset them every visit.
  • Anonymous visit counters — a simple count of page views, kept locally, with no IP address, device fingerprint, or personal identifier attached.
  • Contact form submissions — stored locally so the site owner can review them from the Admin Panel. If the optional EmailJS or Google Sheets integrations are enabled by the site owner, a copy of what you submit is also sent to those services — see their respective privacy policies.
  • Newsletter signup — if you subscribe from the footer, your email address is stored locally for the site owner to see in the Admin Panel. It is not sent anywhere else automatically.
  • Testimonial submissions — anything you submit through "Share Your Experience" is stored locally pending the site owner's review before publishing.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies. It uses your browser's local storage API instead, which is not shared across sites and is never transmitted over the network by itself.

Third-party services

Fonts and icons are loaded from Google Fonts and Font Awesome's public CDNs. A GitHub activity widget (if enabled by the site owner) fetches public profile data from the GitHub API. These requests are subject to the respective providers' own privacy policies.

Your control

You can clear everything this site has stored at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain. Doing so resets the site to its defaults on your next visit.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form.
